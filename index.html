SHADOW SNEAK
SILENT STRIKE

Top-down stealth. Slip between cover, wait for the patrols' vision cones to turn, and strike from behind. Every contract ends with a Boss — take them down, then slip out the exit.

HOW TO PLAY

🖱️ Move: tap / click anywhere to sneak there. WASD or arrow keys also work.

👁️ Vision cones: yellow wedges are what guards can see. If you are caught inside one with a clear line of sight, the alarm meter fills — get out fast or hide in a bush.

🌿 Cover: crates block sight and movement. Bushes hide you completely while you stand in them.

⚔️ Strike from behind: creep up behind a guard (or the Boss) until STRIKE lights up, then tap it, press Space, or tap the guard. A frontal strike sets off the alarm!

💨 Smoke bomb (unlocked Level 3): blinds nearby guards for 5 seconds. Dash (Level 6): a burst of speed.

🎯 Contract: take down the Boss, then reach the glowing green exit. Get spotted 3 times in a level and the contract fails.
